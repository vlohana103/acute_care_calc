Post-TNK Administration Care & Monitoring

Vital Signs & Neuro Check Schedule

  • Q15min for the first 2 hours
  • Q30min for the next 6 hours
  • Q1hr for the remaining 16 hours

Critical Warning Signs

Hemorrhage / Bleeding Risks

  • Acute decline in neurological status or sudden drop in GCS
  • Sudden, severe headache
  • Active bleeding from gums, venipuncture sites, or hematuria
  • Acute hypertension (SBP > 180 or DBP > 105)

Allergic / Anaphylactic Reactions

  • Oropharyngeal or tongue swelling
  • Respiratory distress, wheezing, or stridor
  • Sudden hypotension or hives
