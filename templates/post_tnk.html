Post-TNK Administration Care & Monitoring

Blood Pressure & Oxygenation Targets

Maintain BP <180/105 mmHg and PO₂ ≥95% at all times following administration.

Vital Signs & Neuro Assessment Schedule

Q15 min
for 2 hours
Q30 min
for 6 hours
Q1 hr
for 16 hours

Document VS and neurological assessments at each interval.

⚠ Monitor for Hemorrhage Signs

  • Severe headache (sudden onset)
  • Acute hypertension (SBP >180 or DBP >105)
  • Nausea or vomiting
  • Decreased level of consciousness

If any hemorrhage signs present → obtain emergency head CT immediately.

⚠ Monitor for Allergic Reaction

  • Orolingual angioedema (tongue or oropharyngeal swelling)
  • Urticaria or rash
  • Respiratory distress

If allergic reaction signs present → alert MD immediately.
